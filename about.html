Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't.

NextGR — short for Next Generation — exists to close that gap. We put the world's best scholarships, programs, contests, skills and jobs in one place, match them to each person, and fund prizes that reward ambition.

Ads, sponsors and donors fund our own no-essay scholarships, contest prizes and paid student roles.

How NextGR makes money (and stays free)

We believe in being transparent. NextGR is funded by clearly labelled display ads, sponsors who pay to feature their programs or fund contests, referral fees from partners when students opt in to hear from them, and donations. Paid placement never changes whether a program is listed — only where it's shown, and it is always labelled.

Our promises

  • ✅ Students never pay
  • ✅ We never sell your data — partners only contact you if you opt in
  • ✅ Sponsored content is always labelled
  • ✅ Every listing links to its official source
