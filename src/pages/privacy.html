Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

What we collect

Information you give us: when you use our matching tool, enter a contest, join the talent pool, subscribe, donate, or contact us, we collect the details you enter (such as name, email, country, education level, interests, and optional phone number).

Automatically: basic usage data (pages viewed, device, approximate location, referring site) through cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics and Google AdSense where enabled.

Stored on your device: saved opportunities, your deadline planner and theme preference are stored in your own browser (localStorage) and are not sent to us.

How we use it

  • To send you matched opportunities, reminders and newsletters you asked for.
  • To run contests, verify eligibility and contact winners.
  • To share your talent profile with employers only if you ticked the consent box.
  • To share your details with partner universities, course providers and programs only if you ticked the optional partner-consent box. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • To improve the site, prevent abuse and measure performance.

We never sell your data

We do not sell personal information. Partner referrals happen only with your explicit, optional consent.

Advertising & cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Choosing “Essential only” in our cookie banner requests non-personalised ads.

Children

NextGR is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users under 16 should use NextGR with a parent's or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data, or unsubscribe at any time, by emailing privacy@nextgr.com. Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, India's DPDP Act), you may have additional rights.

Processors

We use trusted providers for forms, email, analytics, advertising and payments (for example Formspree/Web3Forms, Google, PayPal, Stripe). Payment details are handled by those providers and never reach NextGR.

Contact

Questions: privacy@nextgr.com.
