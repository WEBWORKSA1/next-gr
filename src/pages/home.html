FAQ

Questions, answered

Is NextGR really free?

Yes. Students never pay. We're funded by ads, sponsors who pay to promote their programs, partner referrals and donations.

Are the listings legit?

Every listing links to the official provider. We never ask you to pay to apply — and neither should any scholarship. Always confirm the current cycle's details on the official site.

How does the no-essay scholarship work?

Create a free profile and you're entered into that month's draw. One winner is chosen at random from eligible entrants. See the official rules.

I run a program. How do I get listed?

Submit it free. Want more applicants? Promote it as a featured listing or sponsored contest.
